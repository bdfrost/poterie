Soil Jar Test

Find out what kind of soil you have! Healthy soil is roughly 20% clay, 40% silt, 40% sand. This test takes ~1 min to set up and 24 hours to complete.

Instructions:

  1. Fill a straight-sided jar (mason jar) ⅓–½ full with soil dug to root level (~6").
  2. Fill to the shoulder with water. Shake hard for 3 minutes.
  3. Set down. After 1 minute, measure sediment at bottom → that's sand.
  4. After 4 more minutes, measure again. The difference → silt.
  5. After 24 hours, measure once more. The difference → clay.

Amending Your Soil:

🏖️ Sandy soil (well-draining): Add humus, aged manure, peat moss, or sawdust + extra nitrogen.

🌫️ Silty soil (holds water): Add coarse sand (not beach sand) or gravel + compost, or well-rotted horse manure with straw.

🧱 Clay soil (hard to drain): Add coarse sand, compost, and peat moss.

Plants that like "wet feet" thrive in silty or clay soils. Choose accordingly, or amend first!
