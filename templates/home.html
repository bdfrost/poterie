The dramatic centerpiece

Tall, striking plants that draw the eye upward. Examples: Canna Lily, Ornamental Grass, Salvia.

🌿

Filler

Body and fullness

Mounding plants that fill space between the thriller and edges. Examples: Petunia, Geranium, Coleus.

Spiller

Casading over the edges

Trailing plants that spill over container edges or bed borders. Examples: Sweet Alyssum, Creeping Jenny.
