The FST Method

Filler · Spiller · Thriller

What is FST?

The Filler-Spiller-Thriller method is a container and bed design principle developed by professional horticulturists. It creates visually stunning, balanced arrangements by combining three types of plants:

🌺

Thriller — The Star

The thriller is your centerpiece — a tall, dramatic plant that draws the eye and establishes the vertical element. Think Canna Lilies, ornamental grasses, or angelonia.

  • Placed in the center (containers) or back (beds)
  • Usually the tallest plant in the arrangement
  • 1 per container, 1-3 per bed
🌿

Filler — The Body

Fillers provide mass and color between the thriller and the edges. They're mounding, bushy plants like petunias, geraniums, or coleus.

  • Arrange around the thriller
  • Medium height, full and bushy
  • 2-4 per container, 4-8+ per bed
💧

Spiller — The Cascade

Spillers soften the edges and trail over the sides. Think sweet alyssum, creeping jenny, or trailing ivy.

  • Placed at the edges where they can trail
  • Low-growing, trailing habit
  • 1-3 per container, 3-5+ per bed

Pro Tips

  • 🌱 Same sun + zone — All plants in one arrangement need compatible requirements
  • 🎨 Color harmony — Pick a palette (warm, cool, or monochrome) for cohesive design
  • 💧 Water needs — Group plants with similar watering preferences
  • 📐 Rule of odds — Odd numbers (3, 5, 7) create more natural-looking arrangements
